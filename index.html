Daily Recomp Nutrition Plan (Age 36) Muscle Gain + Fat Loss

Built for a morning fasted window, first meal at 12:00, structured afternoon snacks, a planned evening sweet/chocolate treat, and relaxed Shabbat flexibility.
Wake – 11:59 • Morning Fasted Window0 kcal
Water, black coffee, unsweetened tea, or zero-calorie electrolytes. Keeps morning insulin low and frees up your calorie & carb budget for afternoon training, evening chocolate, and Shabbat.
12:00 • Meal 1 — High-Protein Break-Fast~45–50g Protein
3 whole eggs + 150g egg whites (or 200g cottage cheese / Greek yogurt) + 2 slices whole-grain/sourdough toast or 60g oats + chopped vegetables + 1 tsp olive oil.
~15:00 • Afternoon Snack 1~25–30g Protein
1 protein shake (whey/plant) + 1 piece of fruit (banana/apple), OR 170g Skyr/Greek yogurt + berries + 10g almonds.
~17:30 • Afternoon Snack 2 (Pre- or Post-Training Bridge)~25–30g Protein
120g sliced turkey/chicken breast or canned tuna on 2–3 rice cakes/crackers with cucumber/tomato, OR cottage cheese + a handful of pretzels/fruit.
~19:30–20:00 • Main Dinner~45–50g Protein
180–220g chicken breast, salmon, lean beef, or tofu + roasted/fresh vegetables + 150–200g cooked rice, potatoes, or quinoa (higher carb portion on Anterior/Posterior lifting & interval run days; lighter on rest days).
~20:30–21:30 • Planned Evening Chocolate / Sweet Treat~200–250 kcal
Built into your daily calorie target so you never feel deprived: e.g., 30–40g dark or milk chocolate, a chocolate protein bar, or 2–3 squares of chocolate with herbal tea.
Shabbat & Weekend Guideline (Low Restriction)Flexible
Enjoy Kiddush, Challah, Shabbat meals, and dessert without strict macro counting: keep your morning light until lunch, anchor each meal with a palm-sized protein source + salad/vegetables first, enjoy Challah and sweets mindfully, and resume your 12:00 routine on Sunday.
