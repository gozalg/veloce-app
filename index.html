Weekday Routine (Morning Fast → Afternoon Snacks → Evening Sweet)
Morning • Wake – 11:59
Fasted Window (Hydration & Electrolytes)
0 kcal • Supports morning fat oxidation & insulin sensitivity at age 36
  • Water, black coffee, or unsweetened tea / sparkling water (plus pinch of salt or electrolytes on training/cardio days).
  • Keep pre-12:00 intake zero-calorie so your first meal at 12:00 breaks the fast cleanly.
12:00 • First Meal (Break Fast)
High-Protein Anchor + Smart Carbs
~650–700 kcal • ~45–50 g protein
  • Lean protein anchor (chicken breast/thighs, turkey, lean beef, fish, eggs/egg whites, or tofu) + rice, potatoes, quinoa, or whole-grain wrap.
  • Generous non-starchy veggies/salad + 1 tbsp olive oil or avocado for satiety and micronutrients.
15:00 – 15:30 • Afternoon Snack 1
Quick Protein + Fruit / Crunch
~250–300 kcal • ~25–30 g protein
  • Greek yogurt, skyr, cottage cheese, or a protein shake/bar paired with an apple, berries, or a small handful of almonds/walnuts.
  • Keeps afternoon energy steady and prevents a late-day hunger crash before dinner or training.
17:30 – 18:00 • Afternoon / Pre-Evening Snack 2
Light Savory or Protein Mini-Meal
~250–300 kcal • ~25 g protein
  • Tuna or cottage cheese on crisp bread/rice cakes, hard-boiled eggs + cucumber/tomato, or edamame.
  • Bridges the gap so dinner stays balanced and you don't overdo evening treats.
19:30 – 20:00 • Dinner
Protein + Vegetables + Moderate Carbs
~650–750 kcal • ~40–45 g protein
  • Repeatable protein source (fish, chicken, lean beef, or legumes) + roasted/steamed vegetables + moderate carbs (scale carbs higher on lifting/interval days, slightly lighter on rest days).
20:30 – 21:30 • Evening Sweet
Planned Chocolate or Sweet Treat
~200–300 kcal • ~5–15 g protein (optional Greek yogurt/skyr pairing)
  • Enjoy 2–4 squares of dark chocolate (70%+), a small portion of favorite chocolate/sweet, or chocolate paired with Greek yogurt/berries.
  • Budgeted right into your daily target so you never feel deprived—consistency beats strict restriction for recomposition.
Shabbat Flexibility Guide (Low Restriction, High Consistency)
Friday Night • Shabbat Day • Saturday Evening
Enjoy Traditional Meals Without Rigid Tracking
  • Don't stress exact numbers on Shabbat: Enjoy challah, kiddush/wine/treats, family meals, and desserts without feeling guilty or rigid.
  • Simple anchor rule: Start meals with protein and vegetables/salad first, enjoy the foods you love moderately, and leave the table comfortable rather than stuffed.
  • Smooth weekly balance: Because your Sunday–Thursday routine is steady, a relaxed Shabbat fits right into your weekly average—no "punishment" workouts or crash dieting on Sunday.
