Daily Recomp Nutrition Plan (Age 36)Muscle Gain + Fat Loss

Structured around a morning fast, a 12:00 first meal, steady afternoon protein snacks, training-scaled dinners, a planned evening sweet/chocolate, and flexible Shabbat meals.

Morning (Wake – 11:59) • Clean Fasted Window0 kcal
Hydration & alertness without breaking your fast
  • Water (500–750 ml on waking), black coffee, or unsweetened green/herbal tea.
  • Add a pinch of electrolytes/sea salt before morning workouts.
12:00 • First Meal (High-Protein Anchor)~45–50 g Protein
Breaks the fast with lean protein, fiber, and steady energy
  • Protein: 180–220 g chicken breast, turkey, fish, tofu, or 3–4 eggs + egg whites.
  • Carbs & Veggies: Large salad or roasted vegetables + 1 cup quinoa, rice, or sweet potato + 1 tbsp olive oil.
15:00 & 17:30 • Two Afternoon Mini-Meals / Snacks~25–30 g Protein each
Prevents late-afternoon energy dips and supports muscle protein synthesis
  • Snack 1 (~15:00): High-protein Greek yogurt or cottage cheese with berries/nuts, or a whey/plant protein shake + fruit.
  • Snack 2 (~17:30): Tuna/egg salad on whole-grain crackers, edamame, or turkey/cheese roll-ups with sliced veggies.
19:30–20:00 • Dinner (Training-Scaled)~45–50 g Protein
Higher carbs on heavy strength/run days; lighter carbs on rest days
  • Anchor: Salmon, lean beef, chicken, or legumes + generous cooked or fresh vegetables.
  • Carb Adjustment: Full portion of potatoes/rice/pasta on training days; half portion on rest days to leave room for your evening treat.
20:30–21:30 • Planned Evening Chocolate / Sweet~200–300 kcal
Built into your daily budget so adherence stays effortless
  • 25–40 g dark or milk chocolate, a protein chocolate mousse/bar, or a small dessert after dinner.
Shabbat Guidelines • Low-Restriction & EnjoyableFlexible
Enjoy family meals and Shabbat treats without rigid tracking
  • Start meals with protein (fish, chicken, meat) and salads before challah and sides.
  • Enjoy Shabbat desserts/kiddush mindfully until comfortably satisfied rather than stuffed, and resume your normal 12:00 rhythm on Sunday.
